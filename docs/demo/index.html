This page runs WorldNet in your browser as WebAssembly. Nothing is uploaded. Harvest, CheapTrick and D4C analyze a voice once into an F0 contour, a spectral envelope and an aperiodicity. Synthesis then builds a waveform from those three, and the controls below change them before it does.

1. Voice

“His nature must have been kindly.”

Voice: Linda Johnson, recorded for LibriVox, from the LJ Speech Dataset by Keith Ito. The dataset is in the public domain.

Your file stays in your browser. The browser decodes it to mono, at 22.05 kHz where it allows that, and only the first 10 seconds are used.

2. Voice changer

  • Robot gives every voiced frame the same F0, the median of the voiced frames. Whisper makes no frame voiced.
  • Pitch multiplies the F0. Speed resamples the frames in time and leaves the pitch alone.
  • Formant stretches the spectral envelope along the frequency axis. Breathiness multiplies the aperiodicity.

3. Spectral envelope codec

Codec.CodeSpectralEnvelope reduces the envelope of every frame to the number of values you choose, and Codec.DecodeSpectralEnvelope restores it. The audio below is synthesized from the restored envelope.

4. F0 estimators

Harvest ran during the analysis. Press the button to run Dio, and Dio refined by StoneMask, on the same audio and to compare the three contours and their times.
