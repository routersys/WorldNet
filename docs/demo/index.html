This page runs WorldNet in your browser as WebAssembly. Nothing is uploaded. Harvest, CheapTrick and D4C estimate the F0 contour, the spectral envelope and the aperiodicity of the voice below, and Synthesis rebuilds the waveform from them. The pitch shift multiplies the F0 before the synthesis.

Original

“His nature must have been kindly.”

Voice: Linda Johnson, recorded for LibriVox, from the LJ Speech Dataset by Keith Ito. The dataset is in the public domain.
